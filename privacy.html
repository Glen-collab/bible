Footsteps of the Teacher

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026
The short version: Footsteps of the Teacher does not collect, store, or share any personal information — from children or from anyone. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. The app works completely offline.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for or collect any personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, no name, no email, no location, and no contact information required to use it.

How the app works

  • Everything runs on your device. The app does not connect to any server or send any information over the internet.
  • Any progress, badges, or scenes you build stay on your device only. They are never transmitted anywhere.
  • There are no advertisements, no analytics, and no third-party tracking or software development kits (SDKs) of any kind.

Children's privacy

This app is designed for children and families. Because it collects no personal information at all, it complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. We do not knowingly collect any data from children or adults, because we collect no data, period.

Printing

The coloring-page feature uses your device's built-in print function (AirPrint). If you choose to print, your device handles it directly — the app does not receive or store anything about your printer or what you print.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics providers. Nothing about your use of the app is shared with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
